Paronyms

“Contemptuous”: Expressing deep disdain, scorn, or disrespect toward someone or something.
Example: She gave a contemptuous laugh at his feeble excuse, making it clear she held him in utter disdain.

“Contemptible”: Deserving of contempt; shameful, ignoble, or worthy of scorn.
Example: His betrayal of a lifelong friend was a contemptible act that left everyone disgusted.
