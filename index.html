Key Takeaways


  1. What is an inference?
    We know that an inference is an educated guess made by combining clues from the text with general knowledge we already have.

  2. Why do we use inference when reading?
    We know that inference fills the gaps an author leaves, helps us understand characters better, and lets us follow meaning even when nothing is explained.

  3. What is a direct inference?
    A direct inference is a simple, immediate deduction drawn from one piece of evidence. It is also called one-step reasoning.

  4. What is an indirect inference?
    An indirect inference combines several clues to make a complex deduction. It is really a combination of several direct inferences.

  5. What is the difference between an inference and an assumption?
    We know that an inference starts with evidence and follows it forward, while an assumption starts with a belief and bends the evidence to fit.

  6. What is a conclusion?
    A conclusion is the final judgment built from every inference made and graded along the way. It is no longer a guess.

  7. What is the difference between an overreach and a justified conclusion?
    We know that an overreach claims more certainty than the evidence earns, while a justified conclusion names its evidence and would change if that evidence proved false.


Words that I Learnt from the Story 📚

Concatenated
Meaning: Linked together in a chain or series; joined sequentially.
Example: The data files were concatenated into one continuous stream for analysis.

Imprimatur
Meaning: An official approval or authorisation, originally a license to print granted by church or state.
Example: The novel was published only after receiving the editor’s imprimatur.

Congruent
Meaning: In agreement, harmony, or correspondence; in geometry, identical in form and size.
Example: Her values were congruent with the organization’s mission, making her a natural leader.
